# Engagement: EduBridge Bangladesh

## 1. What is in scope?

One screen: the parent-facing tutor booking request (tutor name, subject, verified
badge, qualifications, hourly rate in BDT, single CTA), plus the bKash payment flow
and its confirmation/receipt screen. Card checkout (SSLCOMMERZ) stays as the
existing pattern — not redesigned, just kept as the secondary path.

## 2. What is explicitly out?

Video calling. Search and discovery. Tutor onboarding. Translation of tutor-written
profile content. These are all things that surfaced in the PM thread after the
3-lakh budget was agreed — they're not in this cycle.

## 3. How many revision rounds, and what counts as one?

Two rounds. One round = one consolidated batch of feedback on a single delivered
version. A request for a new screen, a new flow, or something outside this scope
(video calling, badge-issuing logic, etc.) is new scope, not a revision — it gets
its own estimate.

## 4. Who signs off, by name?

Jamie Thornton — to be confirmed in writing. The PM thread shows his manager also
weighing in on decisions (Bengali), so sign-off authority isn't actually settled
yet. This needs a direct answer before delivery, not an assumption.

## 5. What happens when they ask for something new?

Anything outside the scope in section 1 (video calling, the trust badge, search,
etc.) gets a written estimate — screens affected, added time — before any work
starts on it. Nothing new gets absorbed into the existing 3-week/3-lakh budget
without that estimate being agreed first.
